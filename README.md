How to earn money (Basics):
 - Go to the shop that is to your right(Press e to access it), buy a hoe and a seed
 - After buying the items, press e to exit the shop and the items will be lying on the ground to the right of the shop.
 - Use the hoe and right click on an empty grass
 - Right click on the dirt you made. The plant will start growing!
 - After the wheat turns yellow, harvest it by clicking on it
 - To access your stock, press TAB
 - Go to the selling station(Press e to access), and press 1 to sell your stock
 - Repeat!

Things you can sell:
 - Wheat (From farming)
 - Apples (From normal trees)
 - Coconut (From palm trees)
 - Wood (From every kind of trees)
 - Meat (From sheep)
